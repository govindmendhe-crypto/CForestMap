Privacy Policy

Forest Treatment Map Generator • Effective Date: November 2026

100% Offline-First • No Personal Data Sold or Shared

1. Overview

Forest Treatment Map Generator is an offline-first forestry survey and treatment map generation application designed for field mapping, survey grid creation, KML/KMZ/GPX handling, and scaled A4/A3 PDF map export. Your privacy is important to us. The app is built to operate locally on your device without requiring an account or collecting personal data.

2. Location (GPS / GNSS) Data

The app requests foreground Precise Location (GPS) permission solely when you actively use field survey features, such as:

  • Walking and recording forest coupe boundaries or non-working areas.
  • Capturing GPS coordinates for boundary pillars, map edges, and field landmarks.
  • Navigating toward survey points and checking GPS accuracy in the field.

All GPS coordinates are processed strictly on your device. We do not transmit, track, sell, or store your live location on any external server, nor do we collect location data in the background when the app is closed.

3. Local Storage & User Files

Spatial files you import (KML, KMZ, GPX, GeoJSON) and coupe projects you save are stored locally on your device using browser storage (IndexedDB and localStorage). Exported PDFs, KMZ files, and backup ZIP archives are saved directly to your device only when you choose to export or share them.

4. Optional Map Tiles & Network Requests

The core mapping and survey engine works completely offline. If you optionally choose to view or download online satellite/topographic base map tiles while connected to the internet, tile requests are sent directly from your device to the selected public map tile provider and cached locally for offline field use.

5. Data Sharing & Third-Party Analytics

Forest Treatment Map Generator does not include third-party advertising SDKs, behavioral analytics trackers, or user profiling services. We do not sell, rent, or share user data with third parties.

6. Children's Privacy

This application is a professional forestry and field survey utility intended for forestry personnel and general utility users. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

7. Data Deletion

Because all saved coupes, cached map tiles, and preferences are stored locally on your device, you can delete your data at any time from within the app or by clearing the app's storage/uninstalling the app from your device settings.
